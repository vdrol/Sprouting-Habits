Sprouting Habits Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Sprouting Habits is a habit-tracking app. Your privacy matters, so the app is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect
Nothing.
Sprouting Habits does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party SDKs

Where your data lives
Your habits, check-ins, moods, reflections, reminders and progress are saved **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

If you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in those backups. That's handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by Sprouting Habits.

Deleting Sprouting Habits from your device permanently deletes all of its data.

Notifications
If you allow notifications, Sprouting Habits schedules reminders **locally on your device**, at the times you choose. No notification data leaves your device.

Children
Sprouting Habits does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
